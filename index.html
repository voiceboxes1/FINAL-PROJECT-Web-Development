GABAY SA TAGTUYOT

Gabay sa Tagtuyot helps families stay informed, use water wisely, and take practical steps to prepare for changing weather.

What is El Niño?

The El Niño Southern Oscillations (ENSO) is a climate phenomenon that happens over the Pacific and can affect global precipitation and temperatures. It’s more commonly known for its two extreme phases: El Niño, where the ocean’s surface temperature reaches above average, and La Niña, where it reaches below average (L’Heureux, 2014). El Niño is characterized by the drier climate and reduced rainfall. As a result of these conditions, it has been heavily associated with droughts, problems with managing water resources, and agricultural difficulties (de Guzman et al., 2010)

Campaign Purpose

The purpose of this campaign is to raise awareness about the impacts of El Niño and provide information on how communities can prepare for and mitigate the effects of this climate phenomenon.
