Weather and climate information

PAGASA

The Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration provides weather forecasts, flood and hydrological advisories, climate information, and farm-weather services.

  • Central hotline: (02) 8284-0800
  • Public Information Unit: (02) 8927-9308
  • Email: information@pagasa.dost.gov.ph
  • Address: Science Garden Compound, Senator Miriam P. Defensor-Santiago Avenue, Barangay Central, Quezon City, Metro Manila 1100.

Disaster coordination

OCD / NDRRMC

The Office of Civil Defense leads the national civil defense and disaster risk reduction and management program. It also serves as the secretariat of the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council.

  • OCD Central Office: Camp General Emilio Aguinaldo, Quezon City
  • Central office line: (02) 8911-5061 to 64
  • Central office email: cos.ocda@ocd.gov.ph
  • NDRRMC Operations Center: (02) 8911-1406, (02) 8912-2665, (02) 8912-5668
  • Operations Center email: ndrrmoc@ocd.gov.ph

OCD regional phone numbers can change. Check the official OCD website for the current contact for your region.

Your local area

City, Municipal, or Barangay DRRM Office

For local warnings, evacuation guidance, and response, contact your city or municipal Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (LDRRMO), or your Barangay DRRM Committee. Each local government has its own contacts, so confirm the number through your official local government website or city/municipal hall.

Department of the Interior and Local Government · Ask for your area’s official LDRRMO contact.
