Preparation Tips

    National and local governments should:

  • Target and protect food production
  • Prepare water reservoirs and supplies for agriculture and drinking
  • Be ready for other disasters as El Niño goes on (such as earthquakes and typhoons)


    • As for the everyday Filipinos:

  • Store water early in anticipation of possible water rationing
  • Reduce electricity consumption during peak hours to help manage power demand
  • Prepare home emergency kits to address potential droughts and flash floods
  • Support local farmers by purchasing drought-resilient produce and participating in community water-saving initiatives

    • (It is also advised that people prepare emergency food and water that can last for at least three days.)
