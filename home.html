GABAY SA TAGTUYOT

PAGASA reported in September 2026 that a strong El Niño was active in the tropical Pacific and could intensify further. El Niño raises the likelihood of below-normal rainfall and dry spells in some parts of the Philippines, but local conditions vary and rain can still occur.
Note that this is a changing forecast, and it is not a guarantee that every place will experience the same effects.

This campaign aims to help communities understand what El Niño is, how it may affect them, and what they can do to prepare. It is not a substitute for official PAGASA forecasts and advisories, which should be monitored regularly. Gabay sa Tagtuyot helps families stay informed, use water wisely, and take practical steps to prepare for changing weather.


Campaign Goals

  • Help readers understand what El Niño is and how it may affect their community.
  • Share practical, trustworthy preparation tips, especially for water use and heat.
  • Make it easy to find current information from PAGASA and local announcements.
  • Encourage community awareness.
